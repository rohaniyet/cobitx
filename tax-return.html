Common questions

What is the last date to file?

For individuals, including salaried persons, the statutory due date is 30 September following the tax year, unless FBR extends it. Filing after the due date still gets you on the Active Taxpayer List, but only after paying the section 182A surcharge, which the Finance Act 2026 raised from Rs. 1,000 to Rs. 25,000 for an individual (Rs. 50,000 for an AOP, Rs. 100,000 for a company). On top of that, a non-filer pays roughly double withholding on property, vehicles and banking transactions all year. Filing on time avoids all of it.

I have never filed before and have no NTN. Can you still file?

Yes. Registration on IRIS is included in the fee. For a new registration FBR needs a mobile number registered in your own name and a personal email address, because the verification codes go there. Choose "Not registered yet" in the form and we will do the registration first.

Do I have to give you my IRIS password?

No. The password field on this form is optional and we recommend leaving it blank. Once we have your details we will ask you for it on WhatsApp, or we can work with a temporary password that you change immediately after filing. Never send your IRIS password in a public group or to an unknown number.

Why does a wealth statement matter?

Every resident individual filing a return must also file a wealth statement under section 116, and the increase in your wealth over the year has to be explained by your declared income. A return filed without a matching wealth reconciliation is the single most common reason people receive a notice later.

I am overseas. Do I have to file at all?

A non-resident with no Pakistan-source income is not required to file, but many overseas Pakistanis file voluntarily to stay on the Active Taxpayer List — it roughly halves the withholding tax on property transactions, vehicle registration and banking transactions in Pakistan. If you own property or have a bank account here, filing is usually worth far more than it costs.

How long does it take?

Once your documents and fee are with us, most straightforward returns are computed and filed within one to two working days. You see the computation before we submit anything.
