Who we are

About Cobitx

Cobitx Technologies (SMC-Private) Limited is a registered software house in Muridke, Punjab — about forty kilometres from Lahore, in the middle of the rice, transport and manufacturing belt we mostly build for.

That location is not incidental. Most enterprise software sold in Pakistan is written by people who have never stood on a weighbridge or watched a munshi close a day's ledger by hand. We start from the register, not from a feature list, which is why our systems tend to get used after the first month instead of quietly abandoned.

We work with clients across Pakistan and internationally in the USA, UK and UAE. Projects run over WhatsApp, email and video calls, with fixed scope and fixed pricing agreed in writing before work starts.

Do you build ERP software for rice mills and factories?

Yes, and we build them separately rather than selling one generic package. A rice mill system covers paddy intake, drying and milling yield, storage lots, by-products, export batches and costing. A factory system covers bill of materials, batch production, stock consumption, wastage and per-unit costing. Each one is mapped to the registers the client already keeps.

Can you handle FBR tax filing as well as software?

Yes. NTN registration, income tax and sales tax return filing, PRA registration and Punjab Food Authority licensing are handled in-house by a practising taxation professional. Many clients use us for both their systems and their compliance.

How much does a custom system cost, and how long does it take?

It depends on scope, but the process is always the same: we map your workflow first, then give you a written fixed price and a dated timeline before development starts. Smaller systems typically deliver in weeks; a full multi-module ERP takes longer and is delivered in stages so you can use the first part while the rest is built.

Is Cobitx the same as COBIT, the ISACA framework?

No. COBIT is an IT governance framework published by ISACA. Cobitx Technologies is an unrelated software development company in Pakistan. The company name is spelled Cobitx, with an x at the end.
